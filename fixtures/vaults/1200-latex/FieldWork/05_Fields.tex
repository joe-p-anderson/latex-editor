Go for a walk and find an example of a scalar field \textbf{and} a vector field. For your examples, answer the following
\begin{enumerate}
    \item For the scalar field, what would be the physical meaning if the gradient were to have a non-zero negative divergence at a certain location?
    \item For the vector field, what would be the physical meaning if the field had a non-zero curl at a certain location (generally these locations are not points but lines)?
    \item For the vector field, what would be the physical meaning if the field had a non-zero positive divergence at a certain location?
\end{enumerate}
Feel free to sketch some drawings on the back of this sheet.
