Find an everyday example of a heat engine, a machine that compresses gas in a cyclic pattern to extract mechanical work (move an object).

Especially consider:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Where the heat energy input is coming from
        \item What the work is being used to do
        \item Where the waste heat is
        \item Explain how you know that this engine is less than 100\% efficient.
    \end{enumerate}
Reflect on one way in which these considerations change how you view the chosen machine.
