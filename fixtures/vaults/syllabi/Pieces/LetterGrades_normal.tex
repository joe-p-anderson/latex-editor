\subsection*{Letter grade assignment}

\noindent Following are the scores needed to guarantee each letter grade:
\vspace{\baselineskip}

\noindent
\begin{tabular}{cl}
\textbf{Letter grade} & \textbf{Range}       \\ \hline
A            & 92-100      \\
B            & 82-88       \\
C            & 72-78       \\
D            & 65-68       \\
F            & 62 or lower
\end{tabular}%

\vspace{\baselineskip}
Numerical course grades may be maintained through the semester and posted to {\lms}.  Numerical grades posted to {\lms} are meant to provide an approximation of course standing, not final course score, and will be calculated based on the above component weights distribution. The letter grading scheme is subject to change in such a way that the letter grade distribution accurately reflects the level of mastery achieved by students in the class.
