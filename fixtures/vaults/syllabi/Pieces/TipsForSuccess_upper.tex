\section*{Tips for success}

\paragraph{Look for the overarching meanings:} Proficiency in physics is assessed by the ability to apply a surprisingly small number of fundamental principles in novel settings, not in repeating past examples. Quizzes and exams will almost certainly involve scenarios that you have not seen before in class or the homework. When doing homework, pay attention to the patterns of the analysis because these are critical for understanding the meanings of physics principles. 
\paragraph{Form study groups and work cooperatively:} The ability to communicate 
effectively and to collaborate with others is a critical part of doing science.  But be sure you understand the material on your own, too: \emph{the quizzes and exams will expose lack of personal learning. }
\paragraph{Before class:} Read the textbook and look at the assigned homework problems for the day. This will help you to wrap your head around the topic for the day.
\paragraph{Work steadily on the homework:} Do the assigned homework problems for that day before the next class period. If you stay up on the homework in this way, you will be ready to understand the next class period’s content and the homework will not burden you on a single night of the week.
\paragraph{Read the textbook:} Readings for each class are posted in the course schedule below. You must read the material before class and submit a reading report unless told otherwise.
