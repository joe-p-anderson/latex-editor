\paragraph{Attendance}
Regular attendance in class is expected and mandatory. However, in some cases, a student may have a warranted excuse for the absence, including but not limited to: documented illness, family emergencies, call to active military or jury duty, and religious holidays. Students who have such excuses should contact the professor as soon as possible so that accommodations can be made. These accommodations may include alteration of deadlines as well as the recommendation of material which will make up for missed instruction.

\paragraph{Academic Honor} 
Carthage students are bound by an academic honor pledge, detailed in the Student Handbook. Academic honesty means that you must not represent the work of others as your own. This includes not only copying from other students but also ‘found’ solved problems from Chegg or other homework ‘help’ sites, YouTube, or bootleg instructor solution sets. Instances of cheating will result in a non-negotiable grade of zero on the affected work for first offence and failure of the course on subsequent offences. Please review the College guidelines for academic honesty. \href{https://www.carthage.edu/academics/carthage-curriculum/academic-policies/}{Carthage academic policies}

One further note: your grade is not worth your dignity. If you are concerned about your grade in the course, \textbf{please see me in office hours} to discuss honest ways of improving your performance in the course. 

\paragraph{Office hours}
The appointed office hours are times that I specifically reserve for talking to you all about the course material. You are free to swing by my office outside of the designated office hours: if I am in the office, I am likely free to talk to you. I am available to discuss course material, career/life advice, philosophy of science, and most other topics.
