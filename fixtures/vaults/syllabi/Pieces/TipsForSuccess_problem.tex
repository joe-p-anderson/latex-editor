\section*{Tips for success}

\paragraph{Work together:} Work with your partner to attack challenge problems and design experiments. Don't duplicate effort: learn to decide when it would be best to collaborate and when would be best to work alone.

\paragraph{Make suggestions before asking questions:} You never have no idea how to get started. Figure out similarities and differences from physics problems you have encountered in the past.

\paragraph{Use documentation wisely:} Googling is an essential skill in coding. AI tools can also be a useful resource, think of it as interactive documentation from a coding perspective.
